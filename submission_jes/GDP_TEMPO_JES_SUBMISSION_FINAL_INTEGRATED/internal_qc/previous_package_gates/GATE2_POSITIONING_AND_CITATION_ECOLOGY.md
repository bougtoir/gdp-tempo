# Gate 2 — Positioning and citation ecology

## Positioning
The JES version is framed as an **empirically grounded cross-country macroeconomic measurement and accounting study**: how two capital-measurement conventions (constant/negligible investment-to-service lag; incomplete intangible coverage) affect measured capital, TFP and labour shares in 39 (35) economies, disciplined by a flow–stock consistency check against CWON. It is not presented as a forecasting model or as a breakthrough. All adjustments are described as assumption-conditional measurement scenarios.

## Claims corrected
- Removed "all prior work treats the lag as fixed"; time-to-build literature is described as estimating/assuming lags in business-cycle models, with documented lag heterogeneity across regimes and industries acknowledged (Mayer, 1960; Koeva, 2000; Kaboski, 2005).
- Removed any statement that official PIM "everywhere" assumes instantaneous capacity (string 'instantaneous' absent from the JES manuscript); M0 is described as the textbook PIM baseline used in growth accounting.
- What prior work addresses: lag length in cycles; intangible coverage in growth accounting. What we add: time variation and observable construction of the lag, joint with intangibles, checked against wealth accounts across countries. What remains unidentified: structural separation of lag drift from depreciation/coverage; asset-specific drift; true capital.
- Why JES readers care: cross-country TFP and labour-share comparisons depend on these conventions; the observable lag is a cheap sensitivity check.
- Explicit: M2 vs M0 OOS difference not significant (p = 0.18); M4 is flow–stock consistency, not predictive superiority.
